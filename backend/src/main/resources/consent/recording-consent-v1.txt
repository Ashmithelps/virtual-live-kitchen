Kitchen Recording Consent (version v1)

DRAFT — must be reviewed by legal counsel before production use.

By accepting, I agree that:

1. While I am assigned to a kitchen station, cameras at that station may record video of me
   preparing customer orders.
2. Short clips (typically 30–60 seconds) of an order being prepared may be shown to the customer
   who placed that order. That customer may view, download, and share the clip.
3. My display name, photo, and specialty may be shown alongside clips I appear in, and customers
   may send me tips.
4. Clips may be analysed by automated systems to assess kitchen hygiene (for example, whether
   gloves, caps, and masks are worn). These assessments may be shared with my restaurant.
5. I can withdraw this consent at any time from the app. After withdrawal, no new recordings will
   be made of me. Clips recorded before withdrawal may continue to be available to the customers
   who received them.
6. Accepting or withdrawing this consent does not, by itself, change my employment with the
   restaurant.
